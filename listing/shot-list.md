# Photo and video shot list

The judge (3DPrintingDoctor) scores aesthetics and functionality, and he features the best
models in short videos. Real photos of the print matter most. Renders come after them.

## Set-up

- Matte black or dark grey background (black card or cloth). Avoid shiny surfaces: PETG
  reflects light.
- One warm main light from the side (orange or a candle-coloured LED), plus a weak cold fill
  light from the other side.
- Phone or camera on a stand, close to table height. Use the main camera, not the
  wide-angle one, to avoid distortion.
- Clean the parts: remove strings and blobs (a quick pass with a heat gun or a lighter
  for PETG strings).
- Have some small wrapped candies for the stash.

## Photos

| # | Shot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cover.** From the foot end, camera 15–20° above the lid. Lid fully open, the vampire sitting up and looking at the camera. Warm side light on his face. | First image on the page and in the contest gallery: it must show the "make it move" moment. |
| 2 | Closed coffin, three-quarter view from above. The orange cross and R.I.P. clearly visible. | Shows the design and the colour change. |
| 3 | Side view with the lid half open and the vampire half up. | Shows the movement in one picture. |
| 4 | Lid removed, from above: nails in the lid, levers, hip joint. | Shows the mechanism (functionality points). |
| 5 | Lid removed, candies in the stash at the feet. | Shows the "useful" part. |
| 6 | All printed parts laid out flat, plus the coupon. | Shows how few parts there are and that there are no supports. |
| 7 | In the dark, light from below (torch), vampire sitting up. | Spooky mood for Halloween spirit points. |
| 8 | A hand pulling the lid, vampire sitting up (from the side). | Gives a sense of size. |

## Video (15–30 s, record both landscape and portrait)

1. Closed coffin, still for one second.
2. A hand slowly pulls the lid towards the camera. Keep the camera at the foot end.
3. Nothing happens for the first half (build-up), then the vampire sits up.
4. Hold for two seconds on his face.
5. Push the lid back: he lies down again.
6. Optional: lid removed, slow-motion of a nail pushing a lever.

Portrait format works for YouTube Shorts and Instagram, where 3DPrintingDoctor posts.

## After the shoot

- Pick the cover first (shot 1). Crop it to 4:3 for Printables.
- Then upload the renders from `out/render/` (`exploded.png`, `mechanism_*.png`, the
  GIFs) after the photos.
